# Governance

## Today

relais is maintained by its founder, [Pascal Sewonou (@Paskod121)](https://github.com/Paskod121), who has the final say on design and releases while the project is young. Decisions are discussed in public issues and Discussions, and the reasoning is written down.

## How decisions are made

- Small changes: a maintainer reviews and merges.
- Design changes (new protocol, new package manager, anything touching security): an issue or Discussion stays open at least 7 days for comments before a decision.
- Features are prioritised by real reports: packages people and agents actually ran into outweigh opinions, including the maintainer's.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have landed several meaningful pull requests and reviewed others' work can be invited as maintainers. The goal is at least three maintainers from at least two organisations or communities before 1.0, so the project never depends on one person.
